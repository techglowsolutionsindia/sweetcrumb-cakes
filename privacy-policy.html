Privacy Policy

Learn how information is handled when you explore the SweetCrumb Cakes demonstration website.

SweetCrumb Cakes is a portfolio demonstration website. It does not operate as a real bakery or process real purchases, payments or deliveries.

1. About This Website

This website demonstrates the design and functionality of an online bakery website. Product listings, prices, contact details and ordering features may contain sample information.

2. Information You Provide

Certain demonstration features may allow visitors to enter information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, cake preferences and enquiry messages.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, payment credentials or confidential details through demonstration forms. Whether submitted information is transmitted or stored depends on the functionality and integrations enabled on the website.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information entered into website forms may be used to demonstrate enquiry handling, form validation, confirmation messages and related website functionality.

4. Browser Storage

Demonstration features such as shopping carts may use browser storage to retain selected products or preferences. Browser storage can generally be cleared through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, fonts, images or demonstration integrations. Such services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

Reasonable care should be taken when using online forms. However, no website or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security. Visitors should avoid entering sensitive information into demonstration features.

7. External Links

Any external websites linked from this demonstration may have separate privacy practices. SweetCrumb Cakes does not control the content or policies of external websites.

8. Children's Privacy

This demonstration website is not specifically intended to collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Updates

This page may be updated when website features, integrations or information-handling practices change.

10. Questions

For questions about this demonstration website, please use the Contact Us page. The contact information displayed elsewhere on the website may be illustrative rather than an active business contact.
