01 How early should I place my cake order?

We recommend ordering standard celebration cakes at least 24 to 48 hours in advance. Custom or highly detailed cakes may require additional preparation time.

02 Do you offer eggless cakes?

Yes. Many SweetCrumb cakes are available in eggless options while maintaining the same delicious flavour, soft texture and premium finish.

03 Can I customise the cake design and message?

Absolutely. You can personalise colours, themes, flavours, decorations, names, messages and selected finishing details to suit your celebration.

04 Do you provide home delivery?

Yes. We carefully pack and deliver cakes to supported locations so they arrive fresh, secure and celebration-ready.

05 Can I choose the cake flavour and size separately?

Yes. Available flavours and cake sizes can be selected according to the design, number of guests and type of celebration.

06 What should I do if I need a cake for a special occasion?

Explore our occasion collections or contact our team with your event details. We can help you choose a suitable cake for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings and other celebrations.
