How to Choose the Perfect, Birthday Cake

From flavours and serving sizes to colours, themes and personalised details, discover how to choose a birthday cake that feels just right for your celebration.

✦ START WITH THE CELEBRATION

The Right Cake Begins, With the Right Details

A birthday cake is more than dessert. It often becomes the visual centrepiece of the celebration, reflecting the personality of the person being celebrated and bringing the entire theme together.

Before choosing a design, it helps to think about the guest count, preferred flavours, colour palette, celebration style and the little personalised details that will make the cake feel truly special.

Before You Order

  • Confirm the number of guests

01

Choose the Right Cake Size

Start with your expected guest count. A cake that is too small can leave guests without a serving, while an oversized cake may create unnecessary leftovers.

Consider whether the cake will be the main dessert or part of a larger dessert table. This helps determine the most suitable weight, number of tiers and serving style.

02

Pick a Flavour Everyone Will Enjoy

Chocolate, vanilla, fresh fruit and signature flavours all create different experiences. Think about the preferences of the person celebrating while also considering flavours that will appeal to most guests.

For larger celebrations, multiple tiers can also allow you to offer more than one flavour.

✦
SWEETCRUMB TIP

Balance Familiar Flavours With Something Special

A classic crowd-pleasing flavour paired with a more distinctive second option gives guests variety without making the choice feel overwhelming.

03

Match the Cake to Your Celebration Style

The cake should feel connected to the occasion around it. Consider the decorations, venue, invitation colours and overall atmosphere of the celebration.

Soft florals work beautifully for elegant gatherings, while playful pastel details may suit children's birthdays and contemporary textures can create a more modern finish.

04

Add Personal Details

Personalisation is what transforms a beautiful cake into a meaningful one. Colours, flowers, favourite themes, decorative motifs and handcrafted finishing touches can all help tell a small part of the celebration story.

05

Order Early Enough

Giving your bakery enough preparation time is especially important for custom cakes. Detailed florals, handmade decorations, special colour palettes and multi-tier designs require careful planning and craftsmanship.

Ordering early also gives you time to finalise details without rushing close to the celebration date.

✦ THE SWEETCRUMB CHECKLIST

Your Perfect Birthday Cake, At a Glance

Keep these five details in mind and your cake planning becomes much simpler.

01 Guest Count Choose the right serving size.
02 Flavour Pick something everyone will enjoy.
03 Theme Match the cake to your celebration.
04 Personalisation Add meaningful finishing touches.
05 Timing Order early for custom designs.
