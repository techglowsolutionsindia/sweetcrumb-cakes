Delivery Policy

Understand how delivery information is presented on the SweetCrumb Cakes demonstration website.

SweetCrumb Cakes is a portfolio demonstration website. It does not operate as a real bakery or accept actual orders, payments, deliveries or collections.

1. About This Delivery Policy

This policy explains the delivery-related information and features displayed on this sample bakery website. All delivery details are provided for design and demonstration purposes only.

2. Delivery Availability

The website may display delivery options or service availability for certain cakes and occasions. These are illustrative and do not indicate that a delivery service is currently operating.

3. Delivery Areas

Any cities, neighbourhoods, postal codes or delivery coverage areas shown on the website are examples only. No actual delivery locations are served through this demonstration.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery fees, free-delivery offers, minimum order values and promotional thresholds displayed on the website are sample figures. No delivery fees are collected.

5. Delivery Dates and Time Slots

Estimated delivery times, scheduled dates, same-day options and selected time slots are demonstration features. Choosing a date or time does not reserve a real delivery slot.

6. Order Placement and Confirmation

Cart, checkout and order-success pages may simulate the ordering experience. A confirmation displayed on this website does not constitute acceptance of a real order or a promise of delivery.

7. Cake Packaging and Handling

References to protective packaging, freshness, handling or safe transportation illustrate how a bakery might describe its services. No cakes are prepared, packed or dispatched through this website.

8. Delivery Tracking and Updates

Any order-status messages, delivery notifications or tracking information are part of the sample user experience. They do not correspond to real shipments or courier activity.

9. Failed Deliveries and Collection

Delivery attempts, missed deliveries, rescheduling and store collection are not operational services on this demonstration website. Any related descriptions are illustrative only.

10. Cancellations and Refunds

Because no real orders or payments are accepted, there are no actual delivery cancellations or refunds. Please see the Refund Policy for further information.

11. Information Entered During Checkout

Demonstration forms may request contact or delivery information. Do not enter sensitive personal information or payment details. Whether form data is transmitted or stored depends on the integrations enabled on the website. See our Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the demonstration features, delivery examples or website integrations change.

13. Questions

For questions about this portfolio demonstration, please visit the Contact Us page. Contact details shown elsewhere on the website may be illustrative.
